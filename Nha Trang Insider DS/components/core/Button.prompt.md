**Button** — primary brand CTA in five colour modes.

Use for all calls-to-action: story tap-links, carousel slide CTAs, service booking prompts.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" size="lg">Заказать Fast-Track</Button>
<Button variant="coral">Подробнее</Button>
<Button variant="ghost">Смотреть далее</Button>
<Button variant="dark" size="sm">Узнать цену</Button>
<Button variant="secondary" disabled>Скоро</Button>
```

Variants: primary (gold gradient) · secondary (gold outline) · coral · ghost (white outline) · dark (black+lime)
Sizes: sm · md · lg · xl
