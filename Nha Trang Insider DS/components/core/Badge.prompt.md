**Badge** — compact uppercase label chip for service categories, status, and content mode indicators.

Use in post headers, carousel slides, and service listings to label content type at a glance (e.g. "VIP", "FAST-TRACK", "ОСТРОВА").

```jsx
<Badge variant="gold">VIP</Badge>
<Badge variant="coral" size="lg">Fast-Track</Badge>
<Badge variant="lime">Инсайдер</Badge>
<Badge variant="navy">Камрань</Badge>
```

Variants: gold (default) · navy · coral · lime · sea · amber · outline
Sizes: sm · md (default) · lg
